Certified Back4Good® Low-Back & Postural Care

Guided by a certified Back4Good® low-back-pain practitioner, studio cueing focuses on segmental spinal mobility, hip dissociation, deep stabiliser endurance, and relieving prolonged desk or driving tension without rushed transitions.

Every new student completes a confidential pre-class health screening before starting, or can begin with a dedicated 1:1 Back4Good® / Reformer session (£40.00/hr).

Recommended: Functional / Osteoporosis Pilates

Bone-Conscious Functional Alignment & Balance

Specifically structured to support bone density, standing balance, hip and thoracic extension, and everyday joint confidence — carefully avoiding loaded forward spinal flexion or abrupt twisting.

Ideal for clients with osteopenia, osteoporosis, or anyone seeking a calm morning practice focused on upright posture and joint longevity.
